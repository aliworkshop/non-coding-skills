# Follow-Up Cadence

This file says *who* needs a follow-up, *when*, and *what* it says. A card's due date is the
next touch; this file tells you what date to set. For the wording and mechanics, defer to
`email-helper`.

Treat these as default windows, not math to redo each sweep. A date the person named beats
every default here.

## Inbound and cold leads

| Step | Set the due date to | Message |
|---|---|---|
| First reply sent | +2–3 days | — |
| FU #1 sent, no reply | +7 days | A short direct question: *"Did you get a chance to look at this?"* |
| FU #2 sent, no reply | Move to `Lost`, re-engagement date | One line, no greeting, no sign-off: *"Have you given up on this?"* |

## Active pipeline

| Situation | Set the due date to |
|---|---|
| Time proposed, waiting for confirmation | +2 days |
| Call booked | The call date |
| Scope or quote sent | +2 days; longer if they asked for time |
| They named a date ("let's talk in April") | That date |

## Lost

Default re-engagement: **+6 weeks**. Extend when they gave a reason that takes longer: a
timeline ("after Q2"), a competing solution they just bought, a reorg, a known budget cycle.

The re-engagement message is a low-pressure check-in: time has passed, has anything changed?
No reply to that → `Archived`, no date.

Disqualified (never a fit) goes straight to `Archived`.

## Clients and partners (Nurture)

| Situation | Due date |
|---|---|
| Work active | The next session or milestone |
| Work wrapped | ~3 months out |
| Check-in sent | ~3 months out again |
| No contact for 4+ months | Flag it in the sweep regardless of the date |

The check-in isn't a pitch. Its job is to stay close and hear what they're struggling with,
which is where the next piece of work or the next product feature comes from. Anchor it on one
specific detail from the last real conversation.

For a long gap, set the next reconnect on a widening ladder: ~6 weeks → 3 months → 6 months →
12 months. No reply only lengthens the next interval; it doesn't end the relationship.

**Expansion signals** to flag (not act on without Ali's say-so): a new project, a new team, a
budget cycle, "that went well, we want more", a venue asking about more features.

## When not to draft

- The due date is in the future.
- They replied recently and the conversation is live.
- The card is `Archived`, or `Lost` with the re-engagement already sent.
- The only channel is a DM or Telegram Ali sends by hand. List those in the summary under
  "Follow-ups due (DM)" instead of drafting an email.

## Source shapes the tone

- **Referral** — warm; mention who connected you.
- **Inbound** (contact form, email) — interested but cold. Friendly, a bit more structured. Follow
  the cadence strictly.
- **LinkedIn** — often exploratory. Clarify intent early instead of assuming they want to hire.
- **Personal** — match the relationship.
- **Persian-speaking contacts** — write in Persian, in the register they used (see
  `email-helper`).
