# Example: Go Backend Audit Sprint (illustrative)

An illustrative one-pager showing the 7-section structure applied to a freelance offer. It is
not a real offer. Everything in `[brackets]` is a placeholder for a real number, price, or
capacity Ali would supply. Never ship a bracket with an invented value.

---

## 1. Header Block

**Go Backend Audit: A 2-Week Sprint for Seed-Stage CTOs**

For CTOs whose Go API works in staging but slows down or falls over under real traffic, and who
want a backend they can deploy on a Friday without a rewrite or a feature freeze.

## 2. This Is For You If…

- You are a CTO or lead engineer at a seed-stage startup and your Go service is the product.
- You're seeing timeouts or slow pages that only show up under real load.
- You hired a contractor once and got code nobody on the team understands.
- You know there's a slow query or a missing index somewhere, but nobody has a week to find it.
- You can't pause feature work for a month to fix the foundations.
- You're weighing a full-time senior hire and want to know what they'd fix first.

## 3. Core Promise

In 2 weeks, you get a measured list of what's slowing your Go backend down, with the top
[N] issues fixed in your codebase and the rest written up in priority order, so you can ship
without dreading the next traffic spike. No rewrite, no feature freeze, no new stack to learn.

## 4. What You Get

**Core deliverables**
- A load test against your real endpoints so you can see where latency comes from, in numbers.
- Query and index review of your hottest paths so you can stop guessing which query is slow.
- Fixes for the top [N] issues as small, reviewed PRs so your team understands every change.
- A written report ranking everything else by impact and effort so you know what to do next.

**Bonuses**
- Bonus: a runbook for the incidents your service is most likely to have, to cut the time to recover.
- Bonus: 30 days of direct access on Telegram or Slack, to help your team with questions after the sprint.

## 5. How It Works

- **Step 1: Intake.** A 45-minute call and read access to the repo and dashboards. You pick the 3 endpoints that matter most.
- **Step 2: Measure and fix.** Load tests, query review, and PRs for the top issues, reviewed by your team as they land.
- **Step 3: Handoff.** A walkthrough of the report and a 60-day plan for everything not fixed in the sprint.

## 6. Guarantee

If the first 3 days don't surface at least one issue worth more than the sprint costs, I'll stop
and refund the full fee. If by day 14 the measured p99 on your chosen endpoints hasn't improved,
and you've given access and reviewed the PRs, I'll keep working until it has.
